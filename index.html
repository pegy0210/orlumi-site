About the game

A small space that feels like yours.

Orlumi: Petal Nook is a portrait cozy idle decorating game designed for calm, low-pressure play. Begin with a simple nook, collect charming furniture, and slowly shape a place that reflects you.

There is no need to rush. Return for a quiet moment, make one tiny change, and watch your nook become warmer over time.
