# Orlumi Art Direction

## Creative Aim

Orlumi artwork should feel like a living illustrated world: soft, tactile, natural and layered. It may appeal to children, but it must not depend on loud colours, exaggerated cuteness or visual clutter.

## Brand-Level Imagery

Brand artwork should connect multiple worlds without copying one game’s scenery.

Primary recurring motif:

- an ancient, unnamed tree or traces of it
- floating or distant small worlds
- leaves, seeds, branches, roots, wood grain and soft light

The full tree should not be shown or explained by default. Cropped branches and partial silhouettes create scale and mystery.

## Illustration Style

Preferred:

- soft painterly 2D
- gentle texture
- rounded organic silhouettes
- atmospheric depth
- low-contrast shadows
- warm natural lighting
- subtle imperfections that avoid a sterile vector look

Avoid:

- glossy 3D mascot rendering
- hard cel shading
- generic stock-vector scenes
- high-detail photorealism
- intense sparkle effects
- excessive outlines
- overly sharp yellow or flashing highlights

## Composition

- Establish one clear focal point.
- Keep areas of calm negative space.
- Use foreground branches, mist or foliage to create depth.
- Let scenery extend beyond the frame to imply a larger world.
- Decorative details should reward looking without competing with text.

## Colour and Light

- Start from the core brand palette.
- Use sunlight as a soft wash, not a bright flare.
- Yellow accents must remain controlled and low saturation.
- Deep colours may appear in evening scenes but should remain warm and readable.
- Each world may have a secondary palette, while shared neutrals preserve family resemblance.

## Little Explorer Direction

Little Explorer should lean toward:

- curiosity
- discovery
- natural paths and small landmarks
- soft wonder rather than spectacle
- child-friendly clarity without overstimulation

Visual features may include trails, flowers, stones, clouds, small collections and environmental clues. The world should invite observation rather than demand rapid attention.

## Petal Nook Direction

Petal Nook should lean toward:

- intimate interiors
- soft decor
- warm wood and fabric
- plants and small personal objects
- cosy idle atmosphere

Its visual identity may be more enclosed and domestic than Little Explorer, while remaining recognisably Orlumi.

## Character Use

Characters must not dominate the brand homepage unless approved as part of a campaign. The brand should remain capable of supporting worlds with different casts.

For child-facing figures:

- readable silhouettes
- expressive but restrained poses
- no visual aggression
- avoid oversized, hyperactive facial expressions as a default

## Animation in Art

Ambient animation may include:

- leaf movement
- cloud drift
- slow light changes
- subtle floating
- a rare small environmental reaction

Animations must not continuously compete for attention. No rapid sparkle fields or high-frequency motion.

## Image Generation and Outsourcing Brief

Every art brief should specify:

1. page or component purpose
2. focal point
3. required empty space for text
4. target aspect ratio
5. core palette
6. world-specific palette
7. approved recurring motifs
8. prohibited elements
9. export sizes and formats

Do not approve artwork solely because it is attractive; it must fit Orlumi’s visual system and functional layout.
