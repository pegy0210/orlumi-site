# Orlumi Lore

## Status of This Document

This file records only approved shared lore. Absence from this file does not make an idea canon.

## Confirmed Shared Premise

Orlumi contains multiple distinct worlds. They may be connected by a vast ancient presence represented through a tree, but the exact nature of that connection is unknown.

## The Ancient Tree

Confirmed:

- It appears, directly or indirectly, across the Orlumi brand language.
- It may be represented through branches, roots, leaves, seeds, wood grain, light or distant silhouettes.
- Its scale should feel larger than any one visible scene.
- Its public name has not been established.
- Its origin, purpose and complete form remain unrevealed.

Not confirmed:

- that it created every world
- that every world is literally a leaf
- that it is conscious
- that it is benevolent or dangerous
- that it is located in one physical place
- that characters across games understand it
- that all games share one chronological timeline

Do not convert speculation into canon without the owner’s explicit approval.

## Cross-World Connections

Connections should begin as motifs and echoes rather than explicit crossover plots.

Approved forms:

- recurring leaf or branch shapes
- similar wood grain
- distant tree silhouettes
- an old object with an unexplained origin
- a brief line suggesting something larger
- recurring qualities of light or floating particles

Require approval:

- the same named character appearing in multiple worlds
- a shared historical event
- portals between games
- a defined cosmology
- a complete timeline
- a public explanation of how all worlds connect

## Reveal Discipline

A reveal must add meaning, not merely answer a fan theory.

Before revealing shared lore, ask:

1. Does the story need this now?
2. Does it improve the player’s experience?
3. Does it preserve future creative space?
4. Is the reveal consistent across existing titles?
5. Has the owner approved it as canon?

## Family Inspiration

Peggy, Fenwick and Persephone may privately inspire themes of care, protection, growth, spring and curiosity. Their names and biographies are not public canon and should not be encoded through obvious name combinations.

## Canon Levels

- **Level A — Brand canon:** approved in `ORLUMI_BIBLE.md` or this file.
- **Level B — World canon:** approved inside a specific game’s own repository or design document.
- **Level C — Concept:** exploratory idea, not public truth.
- **Level D — Fan interpretation:** not controlled or confirmed by Orlumi.

World-level canon must not override brand canon.

## Current Lore Position

Keep the tree unnamed. Use it as a quiet recurring presence. Allow future stories to decide whether and when its meaning should be revealed.
