# Orlumi Naming Guide

## Purpose

Names must feel native to Orlumi, remain easy to read internationally and avoid unnecessary legal or creative risk.

## Brand and Title Structure

Parent brand:

- **Orlumi**

Game title pattern:

- **Orlumi: Little Explorer**
- **Orlumi: Petal Nook**

Use the full title on first public mention. A shortened title may be used later in the same context when unambiguous.

## Naming Qualities

Prefer names that are:

- easy to pronounce
- easy to spell after hearing
- warm or curious rather than aggressive
- distinctive without becoming complicated
- suitable for children and adults
- flexible enough for international use

Avoid names that are:

- generic fantasy compounds
- difficult clusters of consonants
- obvious combinations of family names
- too close to Orlumi or Lumie
- strongly associated with an existing famous IP
- descriptive claims that may become inaccurate

## Restricted Roots

Do not default to overused fantasy roots such as:

- lumi / luma / lume
- luna / elune
- syl / sylva
- bloom / everbloom
- world tree / tree of life

These are not absolutely forbidden in ordinary descriptive copy, but they should not be used casually as major proprietary names.

## Lore Names

The ancient tree currently has no approved public name.

Do not introduce:

- LumiTree
- a direct family-name blend
- a definitive translated title
- a public “true name”

without an explicit naming decision and documented clearance.

## Family References

Peggy, Fenwick and Persephone may inspire hidden themes, but public names should not visibly combine their syllables. The connection should remain symbolic rather than decipherable.

## Naming Review Process

Before adopting a major name:

1. Define what is being named and how prominent it will be.
2. Check pronunciation in English and key target languages.
3. Search exact and similar spellings across major search engines, app stores, game platforms and social platforms.
4. Review relevant trademark databases in intended markets.
5. Check domain and social-handle implications when commercially important.
6. Record findings and unresolved risks.
7. Obtain owner approval before publication.

A search cannot guarantee legal availability. Formal trademark clearance should be handled by a qualified professional when commercial risk justifies it.

## File and Code Naming

For repository assets and code:

- use lowercase kebab-case for files
- use clear semantic names
- avoid temporary names such as `final-final-2`
- keep internal identifiers separate from public display names

Examples:

- `little-explorer-key-art.webp`
- `petal-nook-world-card.webp`
- `ancient-branch-motif.svg`

## Name Change Rule

When an approved public name changes, update:

- `ORLUMI_BIBLE.md` if brand-level
- relevant specialist documents
- page metadata
- navigation and visible copy
- asset names where practical
- structured data and social previews

Do not leave conflicting old and new names in production.
