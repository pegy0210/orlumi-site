# Orlumi Copywriting Guide

## Voice

Orlumi speaks with warmth, clarity and quiet confidence. The voice may be lightly poetic, but meaning must remain easy to understand.

## Tone Attributes

Use:

- gentle
- concise
- curious
- sincere
- calm
- lightly imaginative

Avoid:

- baby talk
- exaggerated excitement
- corporate jargon
- vague mystical language that hides basic information
- claims that cannot be verified
- forced cuteness

## Sentence Style

- Prefer short to medium sentences.
- Use concrete nouns and simple verbs.
- Use sentence case.
- Keep punctuation restrained.
- One poetic line is stronger than several decorative lines.

## Approved Brand Lines

Primary:

> Small worlds. Gentle discoveries.

Supporting options:

- Discover the growing worlds of Orlumi.
- Different journeys, one gentle spirit.
- Small experiences can still feel meaningful.
- Made with quiet care.

Do not create a new master tagline without updating `ORLUMI_BIBLE.md`.

## Brand Description

Recommended base description:

> Orlumi is an independent game brand creating gentle digital worlds with approachable play, quiet curiosity and room to discover.

This may be adapted by length, but the meaning should remain stable.

## World Descriptions

A world description should explain its distinct appeal in one or two sentences. It must not invent mechanics, characters or release timing.

Current safe summaries:

### Orlumi: Little Explorer

> Step into a small world of gentle discovery, playful observation and curious little surprises.

### Orlumi: Petal Nook

> Decorate a peaceful little nook, collect charming pieces and let it slowly grow with you.

These are provisional marketing summaries and should be updated when each game’s approved product brief is available.

## Status Wording

Use only verified status language:

- In development
- Coming soon
- Available now
- More details coming soon

Avoid:

- launching soon
- almost here
- this year
- pre-register now

unless those claims are confirmed and linked to a real action.

## Calls to Action

Preferred:

- Explore our worlds
- Discover this world
- Learn more
- View on Google Play
- Contact Orlumi

Avoid overly dramatic labels such as:

- Enter the ultimate universe
- Start your epic quest
- Play now and win

## Lore Wording

Lore references should be fragments, not explanations.

Preferred examples:

- A branch older than anyone remembers.
- Some roots never stop growing.
- No one knows where the leaf first fell.

Avoid declaring facts that have not been approved in `LORE.md`.

## Accessibility and Clarity

Poetry must not replace essential information. Platform, status, privacy, support and navigation copy should remain direct and unambiguous.

## Localisation

- Preserve meaning, not English word order.
- Keep official titles unchanged unless an approved localised title exists.
- Avoid idioms that are difficult to translate.
- Maintain a glossary for recurring brand and world terms when additional languages are introduced.
