# Orlumi Bible

> Single source of truth for the Orlumi brand, website and connected worlds.

## 1. Brand Role

Orlumi is the parent brand and the shared home of every Orlumi world. It is not the title of one game and must never be presented as if it belongs exclusively to a single release.

Current worlds:

- **Orlumi: Little Explorer** — in active development; release order is not fixed.
- **Orlumi: Petal Nook** — in development; may release later.

Future titles may be added without changing the core brand identity.

## 2. Brand Promise

Orlumi creates small, thoughtful worlds that invite calm play, curiosity and gentle discovery.

Primary brand line:

> **Small worlds. Gentle discoveries.**

Supporting idea:

> Small experiences can still feel meaningful.

## 3. Core Feelings

Every Orlumi experience should feel:

- warm
- calm
- curious
- natural
- quietly magical
- welcoming
- low-pressure
- memorable without being loud

## 4. What Orlumi Is Not

Orlumi must not become:

- a loud or overstimulating children’s brand
- a neon or high-saturation game identity
- a generic modern SaaS website
- a competitive, urgent or manipulative experience
- a brand built around one mascot or one title only
- a lore-heavy franchise that explains everything immediately

## 5. The Worlds Principle

Use **Worlds** instead of **Games** whenever the context is brand-facing.

Each title is a distinct world with its own tone, visual details and play pattern. All worlds share the Orlumi spirit, but they do not need to share the same genre, characters or exact art direction.

The brand website should make it easy to add, reorder or feature worlds without redesigning the whole site.

## 6. The Ancient Tree Motif

A vast, ancient tree quietly connects the Orlumi worlds.

Rules:

- It currently has no official public name.
- Do not call it a “World Tree” in public-facing copy unless specifically approved.
- Do not explain its origin, age, purpose or full appearance.
- Do not reveal whether it is a tree, a world, a pathway or something else.
- It is a recurring presence, not the main plot of every game.
- It may appear through branches, roots, leaves, seeds, wood grain, shadows or distant silhouettes.
- Different worlds may show different fragments of the same presence.
- The complete form should remain unseen until a future story genuinely requires it.

The tree should create recognition before explanation.

## 7. Mystery Principle

Mystery is created through controlled evidence, not total absence.

Use:

- repeated visual motifs
- fragments
- distant silhouettes
- brief item descriptions
- indirect dialogue
- unanswered but meaningful details

Avoid:

- exposition dumps
- definitive timelines
- naming every element too early
- turning the tree into a secret boss or mandatory central quest
- pretending ordinary missing information is intentional lore

## 8. Family Inspiration

The brand may draw private inspiration from Peggy, Fenwick and Persephone, but their names should not be directly combined into public lore names.

The family connection should remain subtle and symbolic through ideas such as:

- warmth
- protection
- curiosity
- growth
- spring
- renewal
- companionship

This is a private creative foundation, not a public naming formula.

## 9. Design Direction

The Orlumi visual language combines:

- soft painterly illustration
- natural organic shapes
- warm low-saturation colours
- generous breathing room
- gentle depth and layered scenery
- quiet motion inspired by wind, light and growth

Core palette:

- Morning: `#FFF7EA`
- Cream: `#F3EDE3`
- Linen: `#E4D7C3`
- Warm Wood: `#A9876A`
- Sage: `#9FAF8C`
- Blossom: `#D8B7B0`

Additional colours may be introduced only when they remain muted and harmonious.

## 10. Motion Direction

Motion should feel alive but never demand attention.

Preferred:

- slow drifting leaves or light particles
- subtle floating islands
- soft parallax
- restrained hover lift
- gradual light movement
- gentle reveal on scroll

Avoid:

- rapid loops
- flashing
- strong glow
- constant bouncing
- excessive particle density
- animations that compete with reading or children’s focus

## 11. Copy Direction

Voice:

- simple
- warm
- clear
- quietly poetic
- never childish or babyish
- never overdramatic

Prefer:

- “Explore our worlds.”
- “A curious little journey.”
- “More details coming soon.”
- “Made with quiet care.”

Avoid:

- “Play now!!!”
- “Epic adventure awaits!”
- “The ultimate cosy experience.”
- unverified release promises
- claims that all titles share one exact storyline

## 12. Decision Priority

When documents conflict, use this order:

1. Owner’s latest explicit decision
2. This `ORLUMI_BIBLE.md`
3. Specialist guides in `/docs`
4. Existing implementation

Implementation must be updated when it conflicts with the brand bible; the implementation is not the source of truth.

## 13. Change Control

Before making a major brand, lore or visual change:

- state what rule is changing
- explain why
- update this file first or in the same change
- update affected specialist documents
- avoid silent reinterpretation

Last established direction: **Orlumi is a brand home for multiple worlds; the ancient tree remains unnamed and unexplained.**
